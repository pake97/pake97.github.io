Modern AI is an ecosystem of ML predictors, multimodal models, LLMs and agents whose outputs feed each other across heterogeneous, independently governed data sources. Integration alone is not enough: correlational signals invite confounded decisions, and autonomous agents must anticipate the consequences of their actions. We argue the integrated ecosystem needs an **explicit causal layer**, built as a shared, persistent, queryable **Causal World System (CWS)**.
