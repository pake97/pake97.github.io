**Human-Machine Intelligence Integration for Data-Intensive Applications**

Ph.D. in Computer Science · Université Claude Bernard Lyon 1 (LIRIS) · 2023–Present
Supervisors: Prof. Angela Bonifati, Dr. Andrea Mauri

<p><a class="btn" href="{{ base_path }}/files/Amedeo-Pacera-Thesis.pdf"><i class="fa-solid fa-file-arrow-down"></i> Download PDF</a></p>

My thesis investigates how machine intelligence and human expertise can be combined to manage
and repair data-intensive systems. It centers on graph data: predicting where graph data is
hard to repair, deciding when to spend a limited budget on human input, and building interactive
systems that let people steer property graph repair and graph-based causal analysis.

**Topics:** Graph Neural Networks · Human–AI collaboration · property graph repair · budget-aware
optimization · graph-based causal analysis · data-centric AI
